Prompt B:

	Create an artwork using lithography emphasizing texture, lines and shapes of a grand castle sitting by a water body with the background being a dark sky. The final picture should be made using a hand press where the image is pressed and transferred from the surface of a stone to a paper, hence it is a reversed image of the original design drawn. The lithography should emphasize the strong contrast between the dark night sky and the light from the windows of the rooms of the castle to give an idea of the space of the castle as well as the structure of it by showing where the shadows from the lights fall using black and white values.
